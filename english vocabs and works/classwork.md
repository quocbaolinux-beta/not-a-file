# 27/04/2025

## Describe a crowded place you have been to

You should say:

- When you went there?
- Who you went there with?
- Why did you go there?
- And how do you feel about it?

## Examples

1. When you went there:

- Last September, 2024
- During a short holiday trip

2. Who you went there with:

- Went with family

3. Why you went there:

- Heard the Lantern Festival in Hội An is very famous and beautiful

- Wanted to experience it in real life

4. What it was like:

- Town was packed with locals and tourists
- Streets full of colorful lanterns
- People walking, taking photos, releasing lanterns on the river
- Hard to move because of the crowd

5. How you felt about it:

- Overwhelming at first but really loved it
- Magical atmosphere (lights, river, music)
- Enjoyed the food and took amazing photos
- Felt unforgettable despite the crowd
